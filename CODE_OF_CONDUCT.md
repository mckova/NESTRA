# Code of Conduct

NESTRA contributors, maintainers, and users are expected to make participation safe, respectful, and useful.

We welcome people regardless of background, identity, experience, language, or ability. Be constructive, critique ideas and code rather than people, respect privacy, credit others' work, and accept maintainers' decisions about project scope.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, publication of private material, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement should be proportionate, documented privately, and protect reporters from retaliation.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through a non-public GitHub channel. Do not disclose sensitive reports in public issues. Maintainers will acknowledge reports, review available evidence, avoid conflicts of interest, and communicate an outcome when possible.
